Here are some of the photos I took during our three-day trip across Costa Vicentina, the stretch of southwestern Portuguese coast that begins in Sagres and ends in Sines. We drove to Sagres, then over the course of three days explred some of the most well-known and beautiful places that Costa Vicentina has to offer, and finally we returned home. It was a lot of fun, and I hope to continue making these shorter trips to places closer to home.

In this post, I included the complete itineraries for each of the three days. I wasn't sure if it was best to include this post in the Photography page or in the Blog page, but since I'm not going to write much, I'll place it in the Photography page for now.

High quality files can be found here: https://photos.app.goo.gl/u6VNAiauxH8wMQUQ9 (also contains some extra photos!)
